# ✅ Vairi — Trust Through Verified Identity

## 🌍 Product Brief

**Mission:** Build the world’s most trusted identity verification platform that prioritizes clarity, transparency, and user presence over complexity.

**Vision:** Enable authentic digital connections across social media, platforms, and businesses through verification that users understand, trust, and control.

**Core Value Proposition:** Unlike traditional identity verification that hides behind black boxes, Vairi makes verification crystal clear — with real-time trust scoring, transparent user presence indicators, and verified blue checkmarks that bring authenticity anywhere online.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

### 👤 Primary Users
- Everyday Users (18–65): People who want trust in their digital life.
- Freelancers building credibility
- Dating app users wanting verified profiles
- Marketplace buyers/sellers (e.g., eBay, OLX)
- Remote workers establishing trust with clients

### 🏢 Secondary Users

---

## 💡 Use Cases

### Core Use Cases
1. Blue Check Verification → Verified identity badges on Instagram, Facebook, and other social platforms.
2. Profile Trust → Trust badges on profiles across dating apps, marketplaces, and work platforms.
3. Transaction Confidence → Verify identity before high-value or sensitive transactions.
4. Access Control → Secure entry to premium or restricted features.
5. Compliance Verification → Meet KYC/AML regulations with auditable trails.

### Extended Use Cases
6. Cross-Platform Identity → Verified once, trusted everywhere.
7. Trust Recovery → Rebuild digital trust after breaches or fraud.
8. Team Verification → Verify teams for collaborative platforms.
9. Age Verification → Confirm eligibility for age-restricted services.

---

## 🚫 What Vairi Will Never Be

- ❌ No Data Selling → Your identity will never be a product.
- ❌ No Credit Scoring → We verify who you are, not how much you’re worth.
- ❌ No Fake Authority → We don’t replace passports or government-issued IDs.
- ❌ No Anonymous Badges → Verification = real people only.
- ❌ No Social Control → Never a “social credit system” or surveillance tool.

## 🛡️ Data Retention Policy

**Verification Data**
- Identity Documents: Deleted within 30 days post-verification
- Biometric Data: Encrypted + deleted within 90 days
- Verification Results: Retained for 7 years (anonymized after 2 years)

**User Data**
- Accounts: Retained while active + 30 days post-deletion
- Trust Scores: Retained for 2 years
- Audit Logs: 7 years (compliance requirement)

**Communication Data**
- Emails: 1 year
- Support Tickets: 3 years
- Marketing Data: Deleted within 30 days of unsubscribe
